Why BUILDTRACE

Project information exists.
The connection between it often does not.

Construction teams still spend significant effort navigating disconnected records, unclear revisions and fragmented conversations.

01

Latest information is hard to confirm

Drawings, instructions and approvals may exist across folders, inboxes and message threads with no simple project-wide view.

02

Actions lose their context

Teams can see that something is outstanding without immediately seeing the document, discussion or decision that created it.

03

Evidence takes too long to reconstruct

Variation, claim, dispute and handover records often require manual reconstruction from many separate project sources.

How it works

From document upload to traceable project knowledge.

BUILDTRACE keeps the workflow construction-focused while document processing and AI stay in the background.

1

Bring in the record

Upload drawings, RFIs, instructions, reports, correspondence and other project documents.

2

Understand what it is

Extract useful text and metadata, identify document type, date, revision, discipline and status.

3

Connect the context

Link documents with issues, discussions, actions, owners, deadlines and decisions.

4

Retrieve the evidence

Search or ask project questions and follow the answer back to its supporting record.
